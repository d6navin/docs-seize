## What the moderation suite covers

The moderation cog handles everyday enforcement tasks:

- **Punishments:** ban, tempban, softban, kick, mute (timeout), unban, unmute
- **Advanced detention:** jail and unjail with automatic role restoration
- **Warnings:** issue, remove, clear, and list warnings with unique IDs
- **Message cleanup:** purge by amount, user, content, embeds, files, bots, links, and more
- **Role management:** add, remove, create, delete, edit, mass-assign, temproles, restore on rejoin
- **Channel controls:** lock, unlock, slowmode, topic, nuke, lockdown, NSFW toggle, spoiler toggle
- **Server cleanup:** clear invites, strip roles, dump role members, manage emojis and stickers
- **Cases and stats:** view case history, edit reasons, delete cases, and check moderator activity

<Note>
  All moderation commands respect role hierarchy. seize will prevent you from moderating members with a higher or equal top role, and it will not act on the server owner.
</Note>

This page is the full command reference for seize's moderation toolkit. Each command below shows its usage syntax, a short description, and the Discord permission you need to run it.

<Warning>
  Many commands are destructive or affect large numbers of messages and members. Double-check your arguments before confirming.
</Warning>

## Banning and unbanning

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title=",ban">
    **Usage:** `,ban <user> [delete_days] [reason]` **Description:** Ban a user from the server and optionally delete their recent message history. **Permission:** Ban Members
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title=",ban recent">
    **Usage:** `,ban recent` **Description:** Ban users who recently joined the server. **Permission:** Ban Members
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title=",tempban">
    **Usage:** `,tempban <user> <duration> [reason]` **Description:** Temporarily ban a user. seize automatically unbans them when the duration expires. **Permission:** Ban Members
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title=",softban">
    **Usage:** `,softban <member> [delete_history=1] [reason]` **Description:** Ban and immediately unban a user to clear their messages. **Permission:** Ban Members
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title=",hardban">
    **Usage:** `,hardban <user> [reason]` **Description:** Permanently ban a user and prevent manual unban. seize will re-ban them if unbanned. **Permission:** Ban Members
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title=",hardban remove">
    **Usage:** `,hardban remove <user>` **Description:** Lift a hardban from a user. **Permission:** Ban Members
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title=",unban">
    **Usage:** `,unban <user_id>` **Description:** Unban a user from the server. **Permission:** Ban Members
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title=",unban all">
    **Usage:** `,unban all` **Description:** Unban every banned user in the server. **Permission:** Ban Members
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title=",massban">
    **Usage:** `,massban <user>... [reason]` **Description:** Ban multiple users at once. **Permission:** Administrator
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Mutes, timeouts, and restrictions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title=",mute">

  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title=",unmute">
    **Usage:** `,unmute <member>` **Description:** Remove a timeout from a member. **Permission:** Moderate Members
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title=",unmute all">
    **Usage:** `,unmute all` **Description:** Remove timeouts from all muted members. **Permission:** Moderate Members
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title=",imagemute">
    **Usage:** `,imagemute <member>` **Description:** Toggle image restrictions for a member by adding or removing the image muted role. **Permission:** Manage Roles
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title=",reactionmute">
    **Usage:** `,reactionmute <member>` **Description:** Toggle reaction restrictions for a member by adding or removing the reaction muted role. **Permission:** Manage Roles
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
